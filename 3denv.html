12-Month Roadmap

Month 1

UE5 Basics & Blockouts

Master the interface, navigation, and blockout fundamentals.

Software: Unreal Engine 5
Project: First Level Blockout
Month 2

Modular Assets

Learn grid snapping, pivots, and building reusable structural kits.

Software: 3ds Max / Blender, UE5
Month 3

Environment Art Principles

Composition, scale, and storytelling through environment design.

Month 4

Master Materials

Node-based shader creation, vertex painting, and decals.

Month 5

Landscape & Terrain

Using procedural generators for epic background mountains and playable terrain.

Software: Gaea / World Machine
Month 6

Foliage & Nature

Creating and optimizing trees, grass, and scattering logic.

Software: SpeedTree
Month 7

Sculpting in ZBrush

Organic modeling, rock formations, and adding damage/wear to props.

Month 8

Substance Painter Texturing

Baking high to low poly, smart materials, and PBR workflow.

Month 9

Optimization & Performance

LODs, Nanite workflows, profiling, and draw call reduction.

Month 10

Lighting & Post-Processing

Lumen setup, exposure, color grading, and atmospheric fog.

Month 11

Cinematics & Sequencer

Camera animation, focus tracking, and rendering high-quality video.

Month 12

Final Portfolio Project

Create a complete, stunning environment from scratch for your portfolio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience?

No, the course is designed for absolute beginners. We cover everything from the very basics.

What PC specs do I need?

Unreal Engine 5 requires a decent PC. We recommend at least an RTX 2060 (or equivalent), 32GB RAM, and a modern quad-core CPU.

Will classes be recorded?

Yes, all live sessions are recorded and provided to students to review at their own pace.

How much homework is there?

Expect to spend around 10-15 hours per week on practical assignments outside of class hours.

Are there certificates?

Yes, upon successful completion and final portfolio review, you will receive a Revolt Academy certificate.

Is there Discord support?

Absolutely. You get access to a private Discord channel to ask questions, share progress, and network.

Can I get a job afterwards?

The course is designed to get your portfolio to industry standards. Top graduates may also get offers to join Revolt Entertainment.
