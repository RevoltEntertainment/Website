Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience?

No. The course is designed for beginners and starts with the fundamentals of design before moving on to professional workflows.

Do I need to know how to draw?

No. This course focuses on digital graphic design, visual communication, and professional software rather than traditional drawing skills.

Will classes be recorded?

Yes. Every live session is recorded, allowing you to revisit lessons whenever you need.

How much time should I dedicate each week?

We recommend dedicating 8–12 hours per week to lectures, practical exercises, and portfolio projects.

Will I build a portfolio?

Yes. Throughout the course you'll complete branding, marketing, and digital design projects that become part of your professional portfolio.

Is there Discord support?

Yes. Students receive access to a private Discord community where they can ask questions, share work, and receive feedback throughout the course.

What software will I learn?

You'll learn Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign while working on real-world graphic design projects.
